Pitch Video Script & Concept Overview

This video pitches TryLocal from a student consumer's perspective in Mumbai. It introduces the familiar frustration of buying clothing on traditional e-commerce apps—paying ₹2,000 upfront, waiting 4 days, only to find the medium size is too tight at the chest or the fabric color looks faded compared to the photoshoot.

It then transitions into TryLocal: ordering 3 shirts from a boutique right here in Andheri West, welcoming the runner at the door, trying them on in front of the mirror with favorite sneakers, keeping the one perfect fit, and handing the rest back with immediate fee waiver!

Founder Pitch Quote (Meet Gangwal, CEO):

"You wouldn't buy a pair of jeans in a physical mall without stepping into the trial room. Why should you be forced to do that online? TryLocal brings the fitting room to your home."
