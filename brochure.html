Unique Features

Browse verified boutiques within 3-5 km of home and book an exact 45-minute delivery slot. A runner brings 3-5 sanitized pieces so you can try them in your own mirror for 15-20 minutes, feeling the fabric in real daylight. No fit guesswork, no courier surprises.

Special Benefits

No repacking: the runner waits, collects unwanted pieces and finalizes your bill on the spot. Pay only for what you keep, and the trial fee is waived when you buy. Local boutiques gain digital reach and live stock sync without high marketing costs.
