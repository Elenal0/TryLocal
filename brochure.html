HYPERLOCAL FASHION LOGISTICS

TryLocal

"Your Favourite Fits, Tried at Home."

Bringing Mumbai's neighborhood clothing boutiques directly to your doorstep for scheduled home fitting trials.

📍

1. Hyperlocal Store Discovery

Discover verified independent boutiques, ethnic wear, and modern streetwear brands within 3–5 km in Mumbai. Zero wait for cross-country shipping.

🪞

2. Zero-Fit Uncertainty

Try on clothes in the privacy and comfort of your bedroom mirror. Check fabric drape, texture, and authentic natural daylight colors with complete peace of mind.

⏱️

3. 45-Minute Scheduled Slot

No waiting around all day for couriers. Book an exact time window when you are relaxed at home (e.g. evening 6:30 PM).

Did You Know? 60% of online fashion returns occur because of size and fitting doubts. TryLocal completely eliminates pre-purchase guesswork!

OPERATIONS & PARTNERSHIP

How TryLocal Works

Simple 4-phase cycle: Discover → Select → Try at Home → Keep What Fits.

⚡

4. Instant Spot Returns

No repacking boxes or waiting for courier pickups. The runner waits while you try, collects unbought pieces immediately, and finalizes your bill.

🏪

5. Retailer Digitalization

Local Mumbai shopkeepers gain an enterprise dashboard, live stock synchronization, and expanded customer reach without high marketing expenses.

Fast 10-Minute Doorstep Flow:
A Select 3–5 items on mobile app from nearby stores.
B Runner delivers sanitized garment bag at chosen time.
C 15–20 minutes trial in your mirror; decide what to keep.
D Pay only for kept items; trial fee waived if you buy!

Why TryLocal Reinvents Fashion Retail
