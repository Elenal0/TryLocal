Core Ideology

Driven by Purpose & Confidence

Conceived at SPIT Mumbai to solve the biggest friction points in modern e-commerce: fitting regret, painful return logistics, and the decline of physical retail footfall.

Our Guiding North Star

Vision Statement

"To make local fashion shopping more convenient, transparent, and confident by bringing the physical clothing-trial experience directly into customers' homes."

Empowering consumers to evaluate fabrics and fits in real daylight before committing a single rupee.
Operational Commitment

Mission Statement

"To connect customers with nearby clothing retailers through a simple, high-speed digital platform for real-time inventory discovery, scheduled home trials, and seamless purchase-or-return decisions."

Digitalizing Mumbai's vibrant local boutiques and eliminating the environmental waste of multi-day e-commerce shipping.
Patent Specification Workflow

The 10-Step Customer & Logistics Journey

Based on Patent Form No. 2 complete specification submitted at SPIT Mumbai: "Hyperlocal Clothing Shopping System with Home-Based Trial and Automated Purchase-or-Return Management".

1
Enter Location
Customer enters pin-code or selects Andheri West / Mumbai locality to discover nearby boutiques.
2
Identify Stores
Store Matching Engine matches participating apparel retailers within 3-5 km delivery radius.
3
Browse Catalogue
Customer explores live curated collections with verified sizes, fabrics, and clear retail prices.
4
Verify Stock
Inventory Management Engine validates real-time in-store availability and reserves outfits.
5
Select Outfits
Shopper selects 3 to 5 clothing items (or adjacent sizes) to compare and evaluate in person.
6
Book Trial Slot
Shopper picks a designated 45-minute home delivery window that suits their personal day plan.
7
Retailer Prepares
Merchant receives instant notification, prepares items, and packs in TryLocal garment bag.
8
Doorstep Delivery
Hyperlocal runner arrives at customer doorstep with sanitized garments ready for immediate trial.
9
Home Fitting Trial
Customer tries clothes in privacy with their own shoes & mirrors; checks authentic daylight color.
10
Keep or Return
Instant decision: Keep what fits, return unwanted items on spot. Inventory & fee auto-adjusted!

Step 1: Customer Enters Location

103. Location Service

Customer opens the TryLocal app and enters their current pin-code/area in Mumbai (e.g., Andheri West, Juhu, Bandra) or enables GPS geolocation.

⚡ Key Value: Service boundary validation ensures rapid 30-45 min delivery turnaround.

Every item gets its own keep-or-return decision

Unlike legacy courier returns that take 5-7 days and require printed return labels, TryLocal lets you buy 1 item and return the other 3 on the spot. The delivery runner takes back rejected items immediately!

✓ Keep: Bill Kept Items
✕ Return: Collect On Spot

Value Comparison

Online Shopping Today vs. With TryLocal

How TryLocal solves the critical customer pain points documented in Experiment 8: Business Plan.

📦

Online Shopping Today

E-commerce Giants & Fast Fashion Sites
  • ✕ No Pre-Purchase Fit Check: Shoppers must pay full retail upfront before knowing if shoulder or waist measurements fit.
  • ✕ Color & Fabric Mismatch: Studio lighting and saturation tricks lead to disappointment when package arrives days later.
  • ✕ Tedious Multi-Day Returns: Packing boxes, scheduling courier pick-ups, and waiting 7–10 banking days for refund reversal.
  • ✕ Local Boutiques Excluded: Nearby Mumbai stores are completely invisible on massive national portals.
✨

With TryLocal

Hyperlocal Home-Trial Experience
  • ✓ Try Clothes at Home First: Zero financial risk. Try 3–5 items on your own body before spending your money.
  • ✓ Real Item in Your Own Light: Check authentic texture, stretch, and color in your real home bedroom mirror.
  • ✓ Instant Spot Decision & Return: Keep only the pieces you love. The delivery runner takes the rest back immediately.
  • ✓ Discover Local Mumbai Boutiques: Support favorite local retailers across Andheri, Bandra, and Juhu with instant 45-min delivery.
