The 4-Step Experience

How TryLocal Works

Swipe left or right to explore each step of your in-home fashion trial.

Step 01
📍 Local Discovery

Browse & Select Outfits

Pick 3 to 5 pieces from verified boutiques across Mumbai (Andheri West, Bandra & Juhu). Real-time boutique stock without waiting days for shipping.

✨ Zero upfront deposit · Select multiple sizes & colors
Step 02
🛵 Hyperlocal Logistics

45-Minute Doorstep Drop

Our TryLocal runner collects your garments directly from the boutique and delivers a fresh, sanitized garment bag straight to your home at your booked slot.

⏱️ Guaranteed arrival window · Direct store-to-door transit
Step 03
🪞 In-Home Fitting

30-Minute Mirror Trial

Try everything on in the comfort of your bedroom. Feel fabrics, pair pieces with your own shoes and accessories, and see real colors in natural home daylight.

👗 Complete privacy · Zero rushing or awkward fitting rooms
Step 04
💳 Instant Settlement

Keep What Fits, Return The Rest

Pay only for what you love via UPI or card. Hand unwanted pieces back to the runner on the spot with zero repacking, return labels, or courier waits.

🎉 Instant checkout · Trial fee 100% waived upon purchase
