01 Executive Summary

Buying clothes online is undeniably convenient, yet shoppers cannot check fit, size, fabric drape, or true color before paying. Consequently, traditional fashion e-commerce suffers from 30–40% return rates, leading to environmental packaging waste and frozen consumer capital. Simultaneously, neighborhood clothing boutiques across Mumbai possess high-quality, authentic inventory but lack a digital doorstep channel.

TryLocal bridges this gap through a hyperlocal logistics and trial engine. Shoppers browse live inventories of nearby stores, select 3 to 5 clothing items, and schedule a convenient 45-minute home delivery slot. An eco-friendly runner delivers the garments; the shopper tries them on in the comfort and privacy of their home mirror for 15–20 minutes, keeps and purchases only what fits, and returns unwanted items on the spot. The platform monetizes via a small trial fee (waived if a purchase is made) and a 15% commission on completed sales.

02 Abstract & Patent Scope

"The invention relates to a hyperlocal clothing-shopping system that connects customers with nearby clothing retailers through a digital platform. The system uses location information to identify nearby stores and provides clothing products based on inventory information. A customer can select clothing items and schedule a home-trial slot, after which the selected items are delivered by a nearby retailer. The system digitally tracks the trial items and enables the customer to purchase suitable items or return remaining items. The system updates inventory, order and payment information and may adjust applicable trial or delivery charges against the final purchase amount. The invention integrates location-based retail discovery, inventory management, scheduled home trials and purchase-or-return processing."
— Ref: Patent Form No. 2 Complete Specification, SPIT Mumbai MCA Practical

05 Strategic SWOT Analysis

💪 Strengths (Internal)
  • Home trials before purchase eliminate fit uncertainty
  • Easy discovery of nearby high-quality clothing stores
  • Digital shopping with physical in-home evaluation
  • Structured purchase-or-return workflow in 15–20 minutes
⚠️ Weaknesses (Internal)
  • Requires tight coordination between retailers and runners
  • In-store inventory turnover can fluctuate during rush hours
  • Home trial logistics incur localized delivery/collection costs
  • Initial trust must be cultivated with traditional retail owners
🚀 Opportunities (External)
  • Rapid digital growth for independent Mumbai fashion boutiques
  • Direct campus ambassador partnerships across Mumbai colleges
  • Gradual expansion from Andheri West into Bandra and Juhu
  • Value-added retailer marketing & predictive sizing analytics
🛡️ Threats (External)
  • Established e-commerce giants (Myntra, AJIO) discounting heavily
  • Traffic delays during peak Mumbai monsoon or rush hours
  • Potential resistance to standalone trial fees if unadjusted
  • Risks of garment damage requiring clear merchant inspection rules

06 Business Model & Financial Projections

Revenue Streams & Pricing:

  • • Trial/Delivery Fee: ₹200 (Kept only if customer buys nothing; waived upon purchase).
  • • Sales Commission: 15% on the gross value of all completed purchases.
  • • Promoted Listings: Future premium store placements for featured boutique collections.
  • • Target Audience: Mumbai students & young professionals aged 18–35.

Unit Economics (at 300 Monthly Orders):

  • • Conversion Rate: 60% of trial orders result in purchase (180 orders).
  • • Average Order Value: ₹1,500 per converting purchase.
