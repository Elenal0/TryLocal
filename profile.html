TryLocal

Date: Oct 20, 2026
Address: Grace Pinnacle, 1105, Andheri West, Mumbai, Maharashtra
Institution: Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (SPIT), Mumbai (MCA, EIPR)

Abstract: TryLocal is a proposed hyperlocal fashion platform that connects customers with nearby clothing stores and brings the trial to their door. Customers pick clothes, book a slot, try them at home, and keep only what they like. TryLocal aims to give shoppers confidence in size and fit, and to give local retailers a structured digital sales channel.

Startup: TryLocal is a student-led startup concept developed by Aryan Dandekar, Meet Gangwal, Sujal Meshram, and Nupur Thakur during the EIPR course at SPIT, Mumbai. It is currently a proposed concept and not yet an operating business. Each founder leads a core area of the execution plan.

Executive Summary:

Buying clothes online is convenient, but shoppers cannot check fit, size, or colour before paying. TryLocal links customers with nearby clothing stores, delivers the selected items to their home, and collects whatever they do not keep.

How it Works: Customers browse nearby stores in one app, book a delivery slot, and try the clothes at home. Each item gets its own keep-or-return decision, so a customer can buy some items and return the rest.

Revenue Model: TryLocal plans to earn from a trial/delivery fee (initial assumption: ₹200, adjusted against the bill if items are bought) and a 15% commission on completed purchases.

Target Market: Students and young professionals aged 18 to 35 in one limited pilot area of Mumbai.

Roadmap: The plan runs on a 12-month roadmap covering research and planning, development and pilot, local launch, and review and improvement.

Stage: Proposed concept. There is no live product, no customers, and no sales yet. All financial figures are illustrative estimates to be validated through surveys, retailer interviews, and a pilot.

Company Description:
Company Vision

To make local fashion shopping more convenient and confident by bringing the clothing-trial experience to customers' homes.

Company Mission

To connect customers with nearby clothing retailers through a simple digital platform for product discovery, scheduled home trials, and convenient purchase-or-return decisions.

Company Values
  • Customer Confidence: Shoppers should know what fits before they pay.
  • Local First: Nearby retailers get a fair, structured digital channel.
  • Honest Operations: Clear trial and return policies, with no claims that cannot be backed up.
  • Start Small, Learn Fast: Test in one Mumbai area before expanding.
Tagline:
"Your Favourite Fits, Tried at Home."
